About WeatherML

WeatherML combines real-time weather data with advanced machine learning to provide the most accurate weather predictions. Our models are trained on extensive historical data and continuously improved for better accuracy.

Built with Flask, scikit-learn, and modern web technologies, this application demonstrates the power of AI in weather forecasting. Perfect for developers, data scientists, and weather enthusiasts.
